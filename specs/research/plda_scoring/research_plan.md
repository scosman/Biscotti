# Research Plan: PLDA Scoring for Voiceprint Matching

## Goal

Biscotti matches speakers across meetings with stored voiceprints. Two kinds are saved per speaker: "raw" (WeSpeaker-style embedding centroid) and "plda" (SpeakerKit's PLDA-space centroid, exposed by our fork as `speakerPLDACentroidEmbeddings`). The calibration pass (`specs/projects/speaker_embeddings/calibration.md`) compared them with plain cosine distance and raw won. That comparison was probably unfair: PLDA-space vectors come with a per-dimension `phi` (between-speaker variance) vector, and the correct comparison is a PLDA log-likelihood-ratio (LLR) score, not cosine.

This research feeds a benchmark task: implement correct PLDA LLR scoring over our stored voiceprints, compare it to raw cosine on the real store, and decide (1) PLDA helps → fresh branch to do it properly, or (2) PLDA does not help → remove PLDA voiceprints.

The research must answer concretely: what exact formula to implement, which parameters it needs (phi, transforms, normalization), whether our stored PLDA vectors are in the right space to use it, and where to get phi (argmax code/model files) — or whether argmax already exposes a scoring function.

VBx paper 2022). Answer: (a) In the diagonalized PLDA space where within-class covariance = I and between-class covariance = diag(phi), what is the exact closed-form LLR for "same speaker vs different speaker" between two vectors? (b) How to score one test vector against N enrollment vectors of one speaker (by-the-book multi-enrollment: the posterior over the speaker mean shrinks with N; vs averaging vectors; vs averaging scores). (f) How LLR scores are thresholded/calibrated (LLR is in nats; 0 = equal odds; priors). Out of scope: specific codebases (other subtopics).
