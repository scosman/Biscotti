# STT model landscape & Argmax paywall

## Bottom Line

Argmax's paywall covers its **implementations** (Core ML builds of Parakeet and Qwen3-ASR, plus custom vocabulary, real-time, and Sortformer/Nemotron diarization). It does **not** cover the models. Parakeet is CC-BY-4.0 and Qwen3-ASR is Apache-2.0, so a free runtime can use them. The free tier gives only OpenAI Whisper. Biscotti's current model (`large-v3-v20240930_626MB`) is **compressed Whisper large-v3-turbo**, not "non-turbo large-v3" as the repo's argmax doc says. On meeting audio (AMI) the free model is weak: 13.9 % WER, against 9.1–9.4 % for **NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2/v3** and 7–8.3 % for the best 2B speech-LLMs. **Parakeet TDT v2 (English) / v3 (25 European languages)** is the best fit for the swap. It has native word timestamps with about half Whisper's boundary error, native punctuation and capitalization, 0.6B params, CC-BY-4.0, and about 8x Whisper-turbo GPU throughput. On long-form earnings-call audio it equals Whisper turbo but does not beat it. The more accurate models (Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, Granite Speech 2B, Cohere Transcribe, Canary-Qwen) are 2–4x larger. None of them gives native word timestamps: some need a second aligner model, some give no timestamps at all.

## Key Findings

- **Free tier = Whisper only. Pro = Parakeet v2/v3, Qwen3-ASR, custom vocab, real-time, Sortformer/Nemotron 3 diarization.** Pro costs $1–1.33/device/month with a 1,000-license minimum. Argmax Pro SDK 3 (2026-09-23) made Qwen3-ASR its most accurate model, with "NVIDIA Parakeet … the fastest … second highest accuracy". — [pricing](https://www.argmaxinc.com/pricing), [OSS vs Pro](https://app.argmaxinc.com/docs/wiki/open-source-vs-pro-sdk), [Pro SDK 3](https://www.argmaxinc.com/blog/argmax-sdk-3), [Parakeet on Argmax](https://www.argmaxinc.com/blog/nvidia-frontier-speech-models-on-argmax-sdk)
- **Our free model is Whisper large-v3-turbo (4 decoder layers), compressed to 626 MB.** Its `config.json` has `decoder_layers: 4`, and the upstream README labels it "Large v3 Turbo (compressed)". WhisperKit's `_turbo` suffix (e.g., `openai_whisper-large-v3_turbo`) is a full large-v3 with 32 decoder layers. `specs/research/argmax/README.md` gets both wrong and should be corrected. — [README](https://github.com/argmaxinc/argmax-oss-swift), [argmax-paywall.md](./argmax-paywall.md)
- **The 626 MB compression costs a little.** Argmax's on-device benchmark: Earnings22 long-form WER 12.35 vs 11.55 for uncompressed turbo; LibriSpeech 1.96 vs 1.93. — [whisperkit-benchmarks quality_data.json](https://huggingface.co/spaces/argmaxinc/whisperkit-benchmarks)
- **Open ASR Leaderboard (version 21-10-2026), AMI WER (closest to meetings):** Granite-5.0-2B 6.96, Cohere 7.02, Granite-4.1-2B 7.06, Canary-Qwen 7.91, Qwen3-ASR-1.7B 8.31, **Parakeet v2 9.09 / v3 9.42**, Canary-1b-v2 13.03, Voxtral Mini 13.57, **Whisper-turbo 13.88**. Mean of 8 public sets: Qwen3-ASR-1.7B 4.31 (best open model), Parakeet v2 4.70 / v3 4.84, Whisper-turbo 6.36. — [model-landscape.md](./model-landscape.md), [leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard)
- **Long-form tab:** Cohere 9.73, Parakeet v3 10.72, Whisper-turbo 11.01, Parakeet v2 11.18 (earnings21/22 + CORAAL). On earnings-style long audio, Parakeet ≈ Whisper. Parakeet wins on meetings (AMI) and on speed. — [leaderboard_longform](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hf-audio/leaderboard_longform)
- **Parakeet's native word timestamps are better than Whisper's.** FA-Bench (2026-10): word-boundary error about 80 ms for Parakeet vs about 123–152 ms for Whisper, and "Whisper about 150 ms early". A dedicated aligner (Qwen3-ForcedAligner) gets about 31–42 ms. On AMI far-field audio, Parakeet v3 word onsets had a median error of 130 ms. — [FA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32396v2), [TranscribeNext AMI](https://transcribenext.com/transcription-with-timestamps), [features-matrix.md](./features-matrix.md)
- **Parakeet v3 does punctuate.** NVIDIA's card says "Automatic punctuation and capitalization … Punctuations and Capitalizations included". The "no punctuation" concern in the `fluid_audio_engine` draft comes from a runtime or its docs, not from the model. — [parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3)
- **Parakeet v3 detects the language automatically, but its card does not say that it reports the language.** v2 is English-only. Long-form: a 24 min single pass (full attention, on GPU) or up to 3 h (local attention). On-device use will need chunking. — same card
- **Parakeet confidence comes from the decoder, not the model.** NeMo computes 0–1 scores per token or word (entropy or max-prob), so availability and meaning depend on the runtime. The LLM-style ASR models document no confidence at all. — [NeMo docs](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/speech/latest/asr/asr_language_modeling_and_customization.html)
- **parakeet-unified-en-0.6b (2026-04-07)** is only about 0.13 WER better than TDT v2 (5.91 vs 6.04 offline average, on NVIDIA's own test setup). It uses the **NVIDIA Open Model License** instead of CC-BY: rights end if you bypass safety guardrails, and redistribution needs a NOTICE. Its card does not mention timestamps. — [card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-unified-en-0.6b), [licenses.md](./licenses.md)
- **The more accurate but heavier options have timestamp gaps:** Qwen3-ASR (timestamps only through a separate 0.6B aligner, ≤5 min per window), Granite 2B (none; the `-plus` variant generates `[T:N]` tags, ≤3.5 min), Cohere ("does not feature" timestamps or language detection), Canary-Qwen (no timestamps, trained on ≤40 s clips). — [model-landscape.md](./model-landscape.md)
- **License flags:** Exclude the CC-BY-NC-SA `granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc-nc` and the CC-BY-NC Zipformer-XL. Legacy non-English Moonshine models are non-commercial. Cohere is gated (you must log in and agree to share contact details). Parakeet, Canary and Kyutai are CC-BY-4.0: credit the source and state changes. The app already does this for SpeakerKit. Qwen and Granite are Apache-2.0. Whisper turbo is MIT. — [licenses.md](./licenses.md)
- **Not upgrades for batch meetings:** Moonshine (English streaming, AMI about 10.7, no word timestamps), Kyutai STT (AMI about 12.2), Voxtral open models (AMI about 13.3–13.6; the batch Transcribe V2 model is API-only), distil-large-v3.5 (AMI 12.09), Canary-1b-v2 (AMI 13.03).

## Open Questions / Gaps

- **No public benchmark covers long multi-speaker meetings** (1 h, far-field) across Parakeet, Whisper and Qwen3. Short AMI clips are the best proxy. A test set recorded in Biscotti is needed to confirm the gain.
- **No benchmark found compares punctuation and capitalization quality.** Leaderboard WER ignores them. Only IBM self-reports punctuation and capitalization scores.
- The leaderboard's private-data files (Appen/DataOcean conversational speech) are gated. I used my own mean of the 8 public sets instead of the displayed average.
- **Leaderboard WERs are for the original weights (PyTorch, GPU).** Core ML or quantized ports (FluidAudio etc.) may lose accuracy, as WhisperKit's 626 MB variant does. The runtime subtopics must measure this.
- The model cards do not say whether Parakeet v3 outputs a detected-language label, or whether parakeet-unified-en supports timestamps.
- New 2026 Apache-2.0 models (ARK-ASR, Hojo-ASR, higgs-audio-v3-stt, MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize) got only a leaderboard check and a short card read. Their timestamp and confidence support is not verified.
- An extraction tool reported Parakeet's FA-Bench timing bias as near zero. I did not confirm this in the paper text.
